How it works
The same core flow runs across all products — with engineering rigor and pipeline automation built in.
1
Send your specs or RFQ
DGA test values, load profiles, customer bills, or a tender document — whatever starts the job.
2
VoltEdge applies your standards
30 years of design rules, compliance specs, live material pricing, and margin rules run automatically — no manual lookups.
3
Get a winning proposal
Spec, ROI, and bid strategy — ready to submit in hours instead of weeks.
4
The design holds up under real engineering scrutiny
Every proposal is backed by a deterministic calc engine, not AI guesswork — an itemized loss waterfall (soiling, shading, mismatch, cabling, inverter, transformer, each broken out separately), and a real IS 875 wind-load and foundation pull-out check with a pass/fail verdict. When a lender or client's engineer asks "how did you get this number," there's a traceable answer, not a black box.
5
Margin is protected before the bid goes out
Your own quoted vendor rates — not a generic benchmark — drive every ₹ in the cost breakup, with a visible "rates last verified" badge so nothing goes out on stale pricing. Live commodity feeds (silver, copper, aluminium, steel) and automatic subsidy/PLI stacking surface margin most teams leave on the table.
6
Opportunities find you, not just the other way around
A live SECI/NTPC/SJVN/DISCOM tender scanner flags new capacity before you go looking for it. Track a tender and it becomes a pipeline deal automatically — no manual re-entry between "saw the tender" and "we're bidding it."
